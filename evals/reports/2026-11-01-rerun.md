## Accounting and limits

All **80 attempts completed and were graded**, with zero execution or outer-worker errors. Promptfoo completion alone is not a quality score; the separate frozen rubric generated the scores above. Candidate labels were randomized and skill-loading evidence filtered; grading and the subsequent audit were performed by coding models, not independent human reviewers. Original answers and raw traces remain authoritative because filtering can omit mixed-command evidence and grader snippets can normalize text.

Frozen skill, task, rubric and harness hashes stayed unchanged. All trials used one identical global-skill disable configuration, and no comparison variant read a Batman SKILL.md. Both versions had the same 300-second budget and permissions. Review and broader cohorts used concurrency three and two; durations are descriptive. Cached input is included in reported total tokens. Native review reported zero usage counters, treated as unavailable rather than zero cost. The backend-resolved model revision was not exposed.

These are author-controlled development fixtures, not held-out or external benchmarks. Five distinct tasks per cohort and two repetitions do not establish statistical significance or general superiority. Superpowers means its pinned reviewer template running on Codex, not the full framework. No hosted review service or authenticated Claude Code run is represented.

Offline verification passed **28 Python tests and 11 Node tests**. No skill instructions were edited during this rerun. Nothing was committed, pushed or published.
